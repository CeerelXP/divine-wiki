Hello! this is a tutorial on how to optimize models that are more vertices than leagues limit of 65k


<img src="/wiki-images/screenshot-2026-09-22-080130.png" alt="" />

as you can see my 2b model is 219k vertices??? this is way too much for league



First off, youll press tab to go into EDIT mode

<img src="/wiki-images/screenshot-2026-09-22-080143.png" alt="" />


After going into edit mode, press A to select every vertices



Then press M to merge vertcies 

<img src="/wiki-images/screenshot-2026-09-22-080150.png" alt="" />


ALWAYS merge by distance



<img src="/wiki-images/screenshot-2026-09-22-080205.png" alt="" />

It instantly removed that many vertices??? and now we are under our limit with more wiggle room then ever



Another thing you can do is Decimation


<img src="/wiki-images/screenshot-2026-09-22-080529.png" alt="" />



Select your model and then this icon on the right




Click add modifier and search up decimate


<img src="/wiki-images/screenshot-2026-09-22-080718.png" alt="" />



Select  planar and adjust the angle limit untill its to the limit you want, If you mix both decimate and merge it should help out in high vertices model, esp AI generated ones



There you go :D
