Hello! this tutorial will teach you how to use Noskinlite(The thing that makes your mod work on every skin)





First of all, find your Skin0 File in your data folder


DATA/characters/Champ/skins


Find your skin0 bin and right click it


<img src="/wiki-images/screenshot-2026-09-24-125318.png" alt="" />


Select quartz then NoSkinLite


<img src="/wiki-images/screenshot-2026-09-24-125356.png" alt="" />



Multiple files will be made, thats how you know it worked




Something to note is that champs that have 2 data folders like shacosbox/zedshadow etc tend to have their seperate skin0bin, noskinlite them too so they work on every skin


And thats it
