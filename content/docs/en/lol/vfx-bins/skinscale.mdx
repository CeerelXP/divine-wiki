How to change your skins size in bin


Open your bin file using jade<img src="/wiki-images/jade.png" alt="" />



Press Ctrl+f to search


Search up: SkinScale: f32 =


This should appear <img src="/wiki-images/skinscale.png" alt="" />


There you can change the skin scale size, if its too big make it 0.8 or 0.7 

if its too small make it 1.3 or 1.6 this is all prefrence there is no perfect scale, youll have to eye ball it in league untill your happy with the results 


And thats it
