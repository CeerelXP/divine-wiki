---
title: How to add textures to your submesh in jade
---

Ever wonder why your textures always look so messed up or just using The champions normal textures?


<img src="/wiki-images/ugly.png" alt="" />



Thats because League doesnt know what texture to put on your submesh, thats why it appears as white or bugged, heres how to fix it using JADE 




First of all , open your bin in jade<img src="/wiki-images/jade.png" alt="" />

Select your bin file


<img src="/wiki-images/jade2.png" alt="" />


After that select this picture icon on the top to open texture insert<img src="/wiki-images/jade-3.png" alt="" />

Select your submesh on the top, it should say "No match" when it cant find the right file for textures


Select your submesh and the correct texture, it HAS to be .dds or .tex cant be ANYTHING else



After that press add entry, and youre done :DDDD
